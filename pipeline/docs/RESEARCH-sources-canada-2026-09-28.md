## Recommended plan

1. **Now, config only:** drop the 4 dead Serper steps (jobillico, talent, bcjobs, bctechnology) and the 2 generic steps. The remaining 170 credits then go only to the ATS and Wellfound steps. Remove `zip_recruiter` and `glassdoor` from `boards.sites`. Add French and "bilingual" query variants.
2. **Build (S):** SuccessFactors RSS fetcher with the 6 verified BC hosts, plus the Workable global API. Then drop the workable part of the ATS search step.
3. **Build (S-M):** a `searxng` source used as the primary for the ATS/Wellfound `site:` plan, throttled to ≥10–15 s between queries, stopping on CAPTCHA and falling back to Serper (top up $50) or Brave's free 1,000/mo.
4. **Build (M):** GC Jobs (bilingual angle, with a PSEA citizenship-preference flag), BC Public Service (1 request), Getro (3 Canadian VC boards, also for ATS discovery), CharityVillage.
5. **Later:** Talent.com direct, Oracle/Dayforce/iCIMS/UKG generic fetchers once there are enough BC tenants. Resume the slow Workday/ATS tenant discovery for SFU, UVic, BCIT, VCH, BC Hydro and Crown corps.

## Side effect to know about
The SearXNG volume tests tripped Google's CAPTCHA for this server's IP around 18:32 UTC (11:32 PDT). SearXNG suspended its `google` engine for 1 hour, so the agent-search tool had no Google results until about 12:32 PDT. It recovers on its own. Brave in SearXNG was also briefly rate-limited (180 s). No containers were started or stopped.
